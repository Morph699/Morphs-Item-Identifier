Your All-in-One AI Visual Scanner, Creator Studio & Marketplace Identifier

🌟 Overview: 

Morphs Creations Item Identifier is a high-performance, lightweight Progressive
Web Application (PWA / SPA) and mobile camera utility. Built for speed and
versatility. Whether you are scouting thrift stores, verifying collectibles, translating
paper documents, mocking up merchandise, or transforming everyday objects
into 4K digital art, the Item Identifier provides a seamless, immersive
edge-to-edge experience.

🧰 The 10 Dedicated Scanner & Creator Modes

1. 🔍 Visual AI Search

  - What it does: Snaps any real-world object, plant, landmark, barcode, or
    gadget and instantly routes it through high-speed visual search engines
    (Google Lens) for instant identification, related articles, and matching
    visual data.

2. 🛡️ Is It Fake? Authenticity Scanner

  - What it does: Runs captured items (sneakers, designer bags, electronics,
    watches) through a simulated forensic authenticity matrix HUD, analyzing
    stitching, logo vectors, and print consistency to output a percentage
    confidence score.

3. 👕 Smart Apparel & Jersey Scanner

  - What it does: Specially calibrated for sportswear, football kits, jerseys,
    and streetwear. Captures crests, typography, and sponsors, and lets you add
    custom search tags to cross-reference across Google Shopping, Amazon, eBay
    Collectibles, and Fanatics.

4. 📄 Text / Document OCR Scanner

  - What it does: Turn physical paperwork, receipts, labels, and books into
    clean digital text.
  - Key Tools: 1-tap Copy to Clipboard, direct Email to Self, and instant .txt
    File Download.

5. 🎬 Batch Scan Queue Console

  - What it does: Rapid-fire snapping for inventory scouts and collectors.
    Capture dozens of items in sequence into a session tray without interrupting
    your camera feed. Inspect items one-by-one, jump into deep search, and
    return straight back to your queue.
  - Exporting: Export your entire batch queue as structured CSV or JSON tables.

6. 🃏 Custom TCG Art Studio

  - What it does: Turn real-world people, pets, or objects into collectible
    Trading Card Game (TCG) art.
  - Presets: Japanese Holo Anime, Mythic Fantasy MTG, Cyber Mecha, and
    Vintage 1999 1st Edition Card frames.

7. 🎨 Vintage Portrait Maker

  - What it does: Transforms portraits and scenes into authentic historical eras
    using generative diffusion models.
  - Presets: 1920s Film Noir, 1950s Kodachrome, 1970s Disco Polaroid, and 1880s
    Victorian Masterpiece Oil Paintings.

8. 🏷️ What's It Worth? Valuation Engine

  - What it does: Appraises collectibles, retro games, vintage clothing, and
    memorabilia with an estimated market price range and condition tier.
  - Live Marketplace Shortcuts: Search real-time eBay Completed/Sold Listings,
    Amazon Prices, StockX, and Google Shopping Index.

9. 🔮 Re-Imagine 4K Art Studio

  - What it does: Generates high-fidelity 2048p 4K artistic reinterpretations of
    real-world objects using state-of-the-art FLUX models across 8 distinct
    styles (Dark Knight Superhero, Cyberpunk Neon, Anime Manga, Claymation, 80s
    Synthwave, and more).

10. 👕 Print-on-Demand (POD) Merch Mockup Studio

  - What it does: Automatically maps your scanned drawings, logos, or artwork
    onto realistic 3D product canvases:
      - Premium Black T-Shirts
      - White Ceramic Coffee Mugs
      - Sleek Phone Cases
      - Ceramic Dinner Plates & Crocks
