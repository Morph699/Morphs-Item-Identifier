### Morphs Creations AI Image Identifier v1.40

### Short Description

Snap a photo to instantly identify objects, estimate values, catch fakes, and transform reality into stunning 4K AI artwork! Zero ads. No watermarks.

### Full Description

Ever looked at an object, a collectible, or a scene and wondered: *What is that? What is it worth? Is it actually real?* 

Morphs Creations AI Image Identifier (v1.40) your ultimate all-in-one pocket lens powered by advanced artificial intelligence. Built specifically for curiosity seekers, smart shoppers, and digital creators, this lightweight powerhouse turns your device's camera into a brilliant visual search engine. 

Just point, snap, and let the AI break down exactly what you are looking at. From analyzing production details to spot counter-feits, to pulling up instant celebrity filmographies, it handles your visual queries with blistering speed. 

We believe your apps should match your style. Morphs Creations includes a dynamic theme and font engine, letting you swap between 9 high-contrast visual styles (like Matrix Green and Cyberpunk Pink) and custom text sizing for maximum comfort. 

### Key Features

* **Visual AI Search**: Snap a photo to instantly name and discover deep details about any object, item, or item in front of you.
* **"Is It Fake?" Scanner**: Protect yourself from scams! The AI inspects stitching, proportions, logos, and materials to give you a clear confidence score.
* **"What's It Worth?" Mode**: Uncover price estimations and market values for collectibles and items instantly (Lifetime Tier).
* **4K Re-Imagine Art Studio**: Transform your real-world photos into high-resolution 4K masterpieces! Overhaul objects using styles like Claymation, Anime Cell-Shaded, Cyberpunk, or Retro 80s Synth-Wave.
* **Celebrity IMDb Deep Linking**: Instantly identify actors or public figures and jump straight to their official biographies and top-rated filmographies.
* **Batch Queue Console**: Scan multiple images back-to-back without losing your place, then export clean CSV or JSON data logs flawlessly.
